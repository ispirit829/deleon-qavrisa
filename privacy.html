DELEON QAVRISA INC (“we,” “us”) provides Jiggle Rise. This policy describes how we handle information associated with the game and this site. TikTok separately handles information for the TikTok platform under its own policies.

Information we use

Game progress and settings
Personal bests, Daily results, sound settings and cosmetic unlock flags are stored in the game on your device. We use them to remember your progress and preferences. The game does not send these local records to a company leaderboard.
TikTok sign-in, when enabled
We may receive a one-time code from TikTok and send it to our login service, which exchanges it with TikTok for an app-specific identifier and tokens to verify the sign-in. The current game does not request your TikTok profile, photo or contacts through this flow. The login application uses the exchange response for verification and does not write the returned identifier or tokens to a player database.
Ads and TikTok features
If you choose a rewarded video, the game asks TikTok to show it and uses TikTok's result to decide whether to grant a revive. If you use sharing, the game passes a challenge date to TikTok. Shortcuts and profile revisit rewards also use TikTok's features. TikTok may process information to operate those features under its own policies.
Support messages
If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and handle your request. Do not send passwords, identity documents or TikTok login codes.

Service providers

TikTok hosts the game experience and provides in-app services. See TikTok's U.S. Privacy Policy. We do not control TikTok's own data practices.

This informational site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no company-operated analytics, ad scripts, tracking cookies or account forms. Hosting requests may still be processed by GitHub; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our hosting and email providers may process technical information or correspondence to provide their services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to address fraud, misuse or security incidents.

Data retention

Local game information stays on your device unless you clear the relevant data or the platform removes it. We keep support correspondence as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations. TikTok, GitHub and other providers retain data under their own policies and service terms.

Your choices and rights

You can decline rewarded ads and choose whether to share a challenge. Depending on your device and app version, clearing the game's local data may delete your scores and settings. For TikTok account data and settings, use TikTok's tools or contact TikTok.

To ask about information we handle or exercise available privacy rights, such as access, correction or deletion, use the contact below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

Children's privacy

Jiggle Rise is for users eligible to use the platform. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
